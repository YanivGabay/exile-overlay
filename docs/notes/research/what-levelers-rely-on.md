## Q3. Patch facts

### 3.29.0, official notes: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3985332 (VERIFIED unless noted)

**Sockets**
- "Gems can now be Socketed into any Equipment Socket, even if the Gem Colour does not match the Socket Colour."
- "Item Sockets are now always White by default."
- "Chromatic Orbs and Tainted Chromatic Orbs now force one of the Sockets on the item to be Non-White."
- Matching the gem colour to the socket colour gives +10% Quality (as quoted by maxroll F, VERIFIED there).
- "Chromatic Orbs are now rarer, and are no longer sold by vendors… in exchange for Jeweller's Orbs."
- **Effect for the overlay:** socket-colour hunting and the chromatic vendor recipe no longer matter for leveling. Link count still matters.

**Campaign**
- "You are no longer required to free the Scion from the Upper Sceptre of God in Act 3."
- The Act 8 Solaris Temple Level 1 waypoint was moved closer to the Level 2 entrance.
- "There is now a Crafting Bench available in every Town from Act 2 onwards."
- Isla, Niko and Einhar can identify items.

**Quest rewards and vendors**
- All classes can buy **Faster Attacks** from Nessa after The Caged Brute.
- All classes can buy **Faster Casting** from Yeena after Sharp and Cruel.
- Mana-Infused Staff (new Int/Str gem) is a reward for the Templar from Intruders in Black, and Witch and Shadow can buy it from Yeena.

**Mercenaries**
- They appear in areas from Act 3 onward.
- They are **guaranteed in The Sceptre of God, The Grand Arena, The Ridge and The Hidden Underbelly**.
- Temporary campaign mercenaries help for up to 3 areas.
- Their stats scale to the area level, capped at 68.
- Item level is capped by the level of the area where you found the mercenary.
- The four guaranteed mercenaries carry no Rucksack items.
- 3.29.0 Hotfix 3 temporarily disabled mercenary spawns in the Toxic Conduit (https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3988602, VERIFIED via search summary only).
- The 2026-08-05 improvements post lowered enemy mercenary skill damage (https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3994130).

**Gems**
- New: Pact gems (exceptional skills); Coursing Currents and Crystalfall (exceptional supports); Mana-Infused Staff; transfigured Divine Blast of Radiance, Holy Hammers of Spirals, Holy Sweep of Hammerfalls, Reap of Butchery. Dark Pact was renamed Dark Bargain.
- Balance changes to common leveling skills:
  - Flame Dash crit 6% to 5%.
  - Lightning Warp cast time 0.7s to 0.6s.
  - Vortex cast time 0.75s to 0.6s.
  - Cold Snap now has a 25% chance to freeze.
  - Creeping Frost added-damage effectiveness 130% to 90%.
  - Scorching Ray cast time 0.25s to 0.22s.
  - Rolling Magma mana cost -20%.
  - Energy Blade now requires swords.
  - Several DoT skills buffed.

**Passive tree:** new keystones Bitter Frost, Roiling Tempest and Voracious Flame, north of the Witch start.

**Not found in the notes:** changes to XP, labyrinth, passive-quest rewards or flasks. Part of the notes was truncated in fetching, so treat this as "none seen", not "none exist".

**Later 3.29 patches**
- 3.29.0b (https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3989412) and the 3.29.1-3.29.3 patches focus on endgame: Abyss, Voyages and Charts.
- I found no campaign changes (UNVERIFIED, from search summaries only).

**Launch-day campaign bug:** a "The Spirit Flame" progression issue, worked around by crafting an item in the Allflame interface (live thread 3987730, VERIFIED).

## Q4. poe.ninja as a leveling-data source

1. **The builds and profiles API is closed to third parties.** "The builds / profiles API, and every other non-economy endpoint (character, Path of Building, authentication), are internal… not available for third-party use", and clients that misbehave against them will be blocked (https://poe.ninja/docs/api, VERIFIED).
   - The site points developers to GGG's official API application instead.
   - **An overlay must not scrape poe.ninja builds.**
2. **The economy endpoints are public, with conditions** (same page, VERIFIED):
   - Endpoints: `/poe1/api/economy/leagues`, the exchange overview and the stash item/currency overviews.
   - Desktop apps should **proxy through their own backend** with caching and a descriptive User-Agent.
   - PoE1 data refreshes about every 15 minutes.
   - **Usable at league start for:** gem prices (whether to buy or level yourself), leveling-unique prices (Tabula, Goldrim, Wanderlust), and currency values.
3. **Who appears on the builds page:** poe.ninja builds come from GGG's ladder API (the top characters by XP; the docs say to look at "page 750" of the ladder for the cutoff). Players can also add characters through OAuth if they are "high enough level" (https://poe.ninja/docs/character-not-listed-poe1 and https://poe.ninja/posts/dawn-of-the-hunt, VERIFIED).
   - In the first hours of a league, the ladder is made entirely of leveling characters, so the page does reflect what levelers use. The data is only viewable on the website, though.
4. **Time Machine:** "will save a snapshot of the meta every day in the first week and then every following week" (https://poe.ninja/posts/dawn-of-the-hunt, VERIFIED text).
   - That post was for poe2.ninja (April 2025).
   - That PoE1 builds have the same daily snapshots after the August 2025 merger is UNVERIFIED; the builds page renders client-side and I couldn't inspect it.
   - No "leveling" tag or level-bucket feature was found (UNVERIFIED either way).
5. **Implication for the overlay:**
   - Use poe.ninja economy data through a proxy backend.
   - For build and leveling meta, **deep-link** the user to poe.ninja's builds page instead of pulling data.
   - Alternatively, apply for GGG's official API (ladder and character endpoints) and compute "what level 20-60 characters use" yourself.

   - Get a Quicksilver flask (Upper Prison strongbox, or Act 2 Yeena) and 10% MS boots via the recipe.
   - Take the right gem rewards and buy links.
   - Craft the weapon with the Rustic Sash recipe.
   - Hit level 12 by Fairgraves and 16 by Fidelitas.
   - VERIFIED (G, F).
2. **Hour 1-3 (Acts 3-4)**
   - Collect all six normal trials and run **Normal Lab right after Act 3**.
   - Buy gems from the library at level 24.
   - Be level 32 before Kaom's Stronghold.
   - 3.29: pick up the guaranteed Sceptre of God and Grand Arena mercenaries.
   - VERIFIED (K, F, patch notes).
3. **Hour 3-6 (Acts 5-7)**
   - Check resistances before Act 5 Kitava.
   - Collect the Act 6-7 trials and run **Cruel Lab after Act 7**.
   - Keep boots at the next MS tier: 20% at level 24, 25% at 32.
4. **Hour 6-10 (Acts 8-10)**
   - Freeze immunity for Act 8, bleed removal for Act 9.
   - Get 30% over the resistance caps before Act 10 Kitava.
   - Run **Merciless Lab after Act 10**.
   - Move into maps.
   - The early Atlas order matters next; see CyclonDefinitiv's 3.29 Atlas progression video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HppqlC2bDq4 (VERIFIED chapters).
