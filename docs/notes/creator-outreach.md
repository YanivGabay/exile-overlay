# Creator outreach: permission to use names in route presets

Status: **not needed for now** (decided 2026-11-02). Presets get neutral names ("Speedrun", "Safe league start", "Beginner"). Tips are written in our own words, and an in-app "Sources" list links the public videos they came from. Outreach is only needed if we ever want a creator's name on a preset; this kit is kept for that case. Claude never contacts anyone; Yaniv would send.

## What we ask

1. Can we name a leveling preset after you (e.g. "Zizaran-style league start") in Exile Overlay, a free, open-source PoE overlay?
2. Can we link your channel and the video it's based on, inside the app?
3. Optional: would you look over the preset once? We'll fix anything you think is wrong.

What we don't do: copy your text, videos or full routes. The preset is built from tips you show publicly, written in our own words, with a link to the source video and timestamp. If you say no, the preset gets a neutral name ("Fast league start") and your name doesn't appear.

## Message template (short, for Discord or Twitter/X DMs)

> Hi {name}, I'm Yaniv, I make free Path of Exile tools (poetools.dev). We're building Exile Overlay, a free, open-source leveling overlay that follows your zones from the game log.
>
> We'd like to offer a leveling preset based on how you run the campaign (from your public videos), credited to you with a link to your channel. Everything is in our own words with links to your videos, nothing copied.
>
> Is that OK with you, and would you like to see it before it goes out (beta around Nov 13–20)? A "no" is completely fine; then we don't use your name.
