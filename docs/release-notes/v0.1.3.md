# Exile Overlay 0.1.3: gem plan and foldable panels

If you have 0.1.1 or 0.1.2, it downloads by itself and installs the next time you quit Exile Overlay (or use **Restart to update** in the tray menu).

## What's new

- **Gem plan.** Import your Path of Building build and the gem panel shows which gems to take and buy in this act, and from whom: the next two quests with gems, and an **all acts** button for the whole plan. Settings → Guide & build has the full plan as a table, plus the build gems the campaign never gives you (such as Portal). It follows your route options: for example, with the Library quest on, a Witch buys Cleave from Siosa in Act 3 instead of Lilly in Act 6. You can turn it off in Settings → Overlay.
- **Fold panels while you play.** The guide, gem links, passive tree and vendor search fold to a small tab; click the tab to open the panel again. Folded panels are saved with your layout.
- The guide now starts at the top of the screen, clear of your inventory and stash.
- Clicks reach the overlay even with Discord's in-game overlay on, and the overlay never blocks clicks to the game while it's hidden.
- Hovering a panel no longer makes it nearly invisible.
- The built-in guide has the 3.30 passive tree and gems, including the new **Arcane Link** support.
- The [FAQ](https://github.com/YanivGabay/exile-overlay#faq-and-known-issues) covers keyboards whose F-keys open other things (such as the emoji panel on F6).

## Install

New here? Download `ExileOverlay-Setup-0.1.3.exe` below and run it. The beta isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info** → **Run anyway**. [Install notes](https://github.com/YanivGabay/exile-overlay/blob/main/docs/install.md).

Start Path of Exile in **Windowed** or **Windowed Fullscreen** mode.

## Known limits

- League tips marked "unconfirmed" aren't checked in game yet. Guide fixes download by themselves within 30 minutes, no app update needed.
- Windows only, Path of Exile 1 only. Exclusive fullscreen hides overlays (a Windows limit).
